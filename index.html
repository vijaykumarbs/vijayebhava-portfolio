04How I work

From a real problem to useful progress.

A practical sequence for making decisions with the people closest to the work. Start with the problem you need to solve; each engagement can be scoped around your current needs, with the wider process available when it adds value.

01
Understand

Make the real problem visible.

Clarify the customer, business objective, existing workflow and the moments where work gets stuck.

OUTPUT

A shared problem statement and current-state view.

02
Validate

Test what is still an assumption.

Separate what the team knows from what it believes, then identify the most useful evidence to gather.

OUTPUT

Prioritised assumptions, evidence and open questions.

03
Prioritise

Choose where attention can matter most.

Compare opportunities against customer value, business intent, feasibility and risk; agree how progress will be recognised.

OUTPUT

A focused opportunity list and agreed success measures.

04
Design

Turn direction into a usable first scope.

Shape the future workflow, key decisions and exceptions with the people who will use and build the solution.

OUTPUT

A workflow, solution outline or requirements for the agreed scope.

05
Deliver

Keep product intent connected to the build.

Work through trade-offs with engineering and stakeholders, clarify decisions, and refine the solution as it takes shape.

OUTPUT

Clear decisions, delivery priorities and a supportable release.

06
Measure & improve

Learn from what changed in practice.

Review the result against the agreed objective and use real signals to decide what should happen next.

OUTPUT

An outcome review, lessons and recommended next actions.
