<h1 class="wp-block-heading"></h1>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">For years, I did what most of us are told to do.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Study hard. Get a good job. Build a career. Earn well. Keep growing.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I followed that path.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">After an engineering degree and an MBA, I built my career in business analysis, working across fintech and enterprise software. Eventually, I joined Eurofins, where I worked on enterprise products and collaborated with global business teams.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">On paper, things were going well.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">But something didn&#8217;t feel right.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I was spending more time in boardroom meetings where people were trying to prove their points than discussing what would actually make the product better.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I wanted to solve harder problems. Work closer to the product. See the difference my work made to the people using it.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">And somewhere along the way, I realised there was something else I wanted even more.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Freedom.</strong></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Freedom over my time. Freedom to choose the work I take on. And the financial freedom to live life on my own terms.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A high-paying job gave me security. But it wasn&#8217;t giving me the kind of work and independence I was looking for.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">So, I made a decision that wasn&#8217;t easy: I left Eurofins.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Not because I had everything figured out. I didn&#8217;t.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">But I knew I didn&#8217;t want to spend years waiting for the perfect time to change direction.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In January 2025, I began my journey as an independent product manager.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Today, I work with B2B SaaS products, including supply chain and manufacturing software. I&#8217;m also exploring how AI can solve real business problems through practical prototypes.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The journey is still unfolding. Finding the right clients, choosing the right problems, and building a sustainable independent career are challenges I&#8217;m learning to navigate.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">But the difference is that I&#8217;m building something on my own terms.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I&#8217;m not chasing a title. I&#8217;m trying to build a career where the problems are worth solving, the work makes a difference, and I have more control over how I live.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I don&#8217;t have all the answers yet.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">But I&#8217;m finally asking myself a different question:</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>What kind of work and life do I actually want to build?</strong></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">And I&#8217;m going to find out, one problem at a time.</p>
